I completed my M.S. in Robotics at the University of California, Riverside in December 2025 and now work as a research assistant in its Motion Planning Lab, focusing on embodied AI, machine learning, motion planning, and social robot navigation. My recent work spans crowd simulation for robot navigation, human-in-the-loop autonomous driving experiments, and reinforcement learning for sequential decision-making.

At UC Riverside, I have worked under the supervision of [Prof. Ioannis Karamouzas](https://www.cs.ucr.edu/~ioannis/) in the [Motion Planning Lab](https://motion-lab.github.io/) and [Prof. Hang Qiu](https://hangqiu.github.io/) in the [Collaborative Intelligence Systems Lab](https://cisl.ucr.edu/), on platforms for reproducible robotics evaluation and multimodal autonomous driving data collection. Before graduate school, I worked as a software developer at Vijeta Transport and as a systems engineer at Tata Consultancy Services, building production software, ETL workflows, and cloud data pipelines.

I am especially interested in robot learning, embodied intelligence, simulation-driven evaluation, and real-world autonomy. My coursework and projects have covered reinforcement learning, computer vision, natural language processing, warehouse robotics, and demonstration-augmented policy optimization.
